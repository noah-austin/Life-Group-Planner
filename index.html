How the rotation works

We cycle through four weeks: Men’s night, Women’s night, All together, then a Fun meetup out somewhere.

Hosting rotates between the Austin’s, Havlin’s, Smith’s, and Johnson’s. Fun weeks don’t use a hosting turn — the next family up hosts the following week.

Leading rotates too: Noah A, Mitchell, Pablo & Noah J take turns on men’s nights, Jillian, Emmalie, Haleigh & Carly on women’s nights, and the families take turns on all-together nights. The rotations are offset so nobody ever hosts and leads the same week.

Anyone in the group can tap ✎ Edit schedule above to make changes — they save for everyone.
