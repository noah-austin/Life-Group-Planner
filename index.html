How the rotation works

We cycle through four weeks: Men’s night, Women’s night, All together, then a Fun meetup out somewhere.

Hosting rotates between the Austin’s, Havlin’s, Smith’s, and Johnson’s. Fun weeks don’t use a hosting turn — the next family up hosts the following week.

Leading rotates too: Noah A, Mitchell, Pablo & Noah J on men’s nights, Jillian, Emmalie, Haleigh & Carly on women’s nights, and the families on all-together nights. Nobody ever hosts and leads the same week — if a change would cause that, the rotation skips them to their next turn.

Anyone in the group can tap ✎ Edit schedule above. A change can apply to just that week, or shift the whole rotation from that week forward. The ⟳ mark shows where a rotation shift starts; the orange dot marks a one-week change.
