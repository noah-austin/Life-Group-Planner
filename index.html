Life Group

Wednesday nights · who, where & when

How the rotation works

We meet every Wednesday at 6:00 PM, alternating All together nights and Split nights, with a Fun meetup out somewhere every fifth week. On split nights the men and women meet separately.

The Johnson’s host every split night, and the Austin’s, Havlin’s & Smith’s take turns hosting together nights. Leading rotates by family — on split nights the husband leads the men and the wife leads the women. Over each 15 weeks every family leads three times, and nobody ever hosts and leads the same night.

Anyone can tap ✎ Edit schedule above to make changes — they save for everyone. A change can apply to just that week, or shift the whole rotation from that week forward. The ⟳ mark shows where a rotation shift starts; the orange dot marks a one-week change.

Planning something extra together? While editing, tap + Add event to put a one-off event on the schedule — any day of the week.
